Frequently Asked Questions

What is My Little Reality?

My Little Reality is a creative self-reflection app featuring affirmations, guided exercises, immersive stories, and magazine-style content. It offers a quiet space to explore your thoughts and imagine new possibilities.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then select Subscriptions. Find My Little Reality to manage or cancel your subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current subscription period.

How does the free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers may receive a 3-day free trial. The subscription automatically renews at the displayed price unless cancelled. Trial eligibility and billing details are shown before purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open My Little Reality and use the Restore Purchases option where available. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If you still need help, contact us by email.

How can I request a refund?

Purchases made through Apple's App Store are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com .

Is My Little Reality a mental health service?

No. My Little Reality provides creative content and exercises for personal reflection. It does not provide therapy, medical advice, or guaranteed outcomes, and is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
