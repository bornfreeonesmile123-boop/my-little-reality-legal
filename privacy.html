Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

My Little Reality ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal reflection and content app operated by an individual developer based in mainland China. Contact: mylittlereality.support@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores personal collections, saved items, user-created affirmations, drafts, and related preferences or reading information locally using device storage, including SQLite and AsyncStorage. Some exercises may remain in memory only during a session. We do not intentionally upload the contents of your personal collections or drafts to our servers through these features.

2. Content delivery

The App uses Supabase to retrieve editorial content such as affirmations, Mental Diet prompts, stories, and magazine issues. When you access content, Supabase processes and logs technical information associated with your requests, including your IP address, approximate geographic information inferred from your IP address (such as country, region, and city), app and device software information, request timestamps, and request metadata. This information is used to deliver content, maintain service availability, and support security and abuse prevention. We do not use this information for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

The App uses Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlements. Apple processes payment information. RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, app identifiers, and related technical information needed to provide subscription services. We do not receive your full payment card details through the App.

4. Exporting and sharing

You can initiate exports of your saved content as TXT, PDF, or a JSON backup. JSON backups can contain collection items, drafts, and favorite relationships, including records that are not currently displayed as favorites. Exports create temporary files on your device and open the operating system's share sheet. You choose where to save or send them. Once shared with another app, cloud storage service, or person, their handling of the files is governed by their own practices. Exported files may remain in the App's cache or at destinations you select.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local records generally remain on your device until you remove them using available App controls, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to device backup and operating-system behavior. Removing an item from favorites does not necessarily delete the underlying created affirmation. Copies you exported or shared are not automatically deleted when you delete local content. Subscription records are maintained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own retention practices. Technical request logs processed by Supabase are retained and managed according to the provider's applicable logging, retention, and security practices. We do not control all retention periods for independently operated third-party services.

6. Third-party services

The App integrates Apple App Store in-app purchases, RevenueCat, and Supabase. These providers may process technical and transaction-related information under their own privacy policies. The App does not currently include an advertising SDK in the dependency list reviewed. This statement does not rule out operating-system or service-provider diagnostics.

7. Security

We use local device storage for personal content and rely on the security controls of the device and the services involved. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please take care when exporting personal writing to other destinations.

8. Your choices and rights

You may manage local content using the App's available controls, export your data, and manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of, or information about personal data we control, and to raise concerns with a relevant regulator. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law. Some device-only content is not accessible to us. Contact us at mylittlereality.support@gmail.com for privacy requests. We may need to refer requests concerning Apple or other independently operated services to the relevant provider.

9. Children

My Little Reality is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. You must be at least 18 years old to use the App. The App is not designed or offered for use by individuals under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from minors. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to us, please contact us at mylittlereality.support@gmail.com.

10. International processing

Third-party service providers may process technical and purchase information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable policies and safeguards.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The updated effective date will appear above. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.

12. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact mylittlereality.support@gmail.com.
